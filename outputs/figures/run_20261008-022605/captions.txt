Fig. 1. Macro-AUROC over the five diagnostic superclasses on the test fold (fold 10). (a) Metadata-only models trained on folds 1–9 (post hoc); gradient boosting values and intervals are means over three seeds. Acquisition metadata: recording device, site and nurse. (b) ECG networks (three-seed ensembles) without metadata, with age and sex, and with age, sex and acquisition metadata. Horizontal lines: 95% patient-level bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples); in (b) they are narrower than the markers. Vertical line: chance level.

Fig. 2. Change in test-fold macro-AUROC. Left: adding acquisition metadata to an ECG network that already receives age and sex (pre-registered H3; ResNet1d-Wang primary, Inception1d robustness). Middle: adding age and sex to the ECG-only network (exploratory). Right: correct minus shuffled acquisition metadata, mean (hollow marker) and range over 20 shuffles (exploratory). Thick and thin bars: 90% and 95% patient-level bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples). Shaded band: ±0.01, the pre-registered smallest effect size of interest for H3.
